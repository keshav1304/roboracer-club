How to

Click empty space to add a waypoint at the end. Click on the line to insert one there.

Drag a waypoint to move it. Right-click one to delete it.

Drag empty space to pan, scroll to zoom. Ctrl/⌘ Z undo.

Waypoint 1 (larger) is the start; arrows show driving direction. The loop closes automatically.
